Eight public tools

Plain descriptions. Schemas are available from the machine manifest.

  • arif_initStart a governed session. Binds actor, floors and audit before other verbs.
  • arif_observeSense reality into evidence — not judgment, not mutation.
  • arif_thinkStructure reasoning under truth and humility floors.
  • arif_routeSend intent to the right organ (GEOX, WEALTH, WELL, A-FORGE).
  • arif_memoryGoverned memory across the federation stack.
  • arif_judgeConstitutional verdict: SEAL, HOLD, SABAR or VOID.
  • arif_forgeExecution gate through A-FORGE — only after SEAL + lease.
  • arif_sealAppend an immutable receipt to VAULT999.

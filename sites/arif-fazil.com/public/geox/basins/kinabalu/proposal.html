01

Executive Argument

Kinabalu Basin is not a simple post-rift sag or a collision product. It is the compressional and sediment-routing expression of a Proto South China Sea subduction system that drove carbonate platform development, fold-thrust architecture, and deepwater fairways — all from the same machine, in sequence.

The basin records six distinct tectonic episodes spanning ~180 Ma to present. The Miocene carbonate play — the primary exploration target — is defined not by when carbonates grew fastest, but by when they survived: a geomechanical stack of serpentinite, carbonate, and fold-thrust-belt clastics decoupled the reservoir from tectonic destruction. That survival window closes at the Middle Miocene Unconformity (~16 Ma), driven by the earlier collision at ~23 Ma (BMU/TCU), not by the Deep Regional Unconformity.

Material correction (NSPW Synthesis 2026-07-16): DRU (~15 Ma) is the slab breakoff and wedge-top response — not the collision unconformity. BMU/TCU at ~23 Ma is collision onset. This shifts the causal interpretation of carbonate drowning: the window closes because collision (BMU/TCU) propagated into the shelf over ~7 Ma. DRU is a second-order response 8 Ma later. Any timing argument built on DRU as the collision marker is structurally wrong.

Basement status: The basement nature beneath the basin remains unresolved. Four competing hypotheses are alive with roughly equal prior probability. None can be promoted to diagnostic without targeted acquisition. The cheapest discriminating test — depth conversion of the KT-7 reflector — is available today using existing data.

Stratigraphic Inference

Eight tectono-stratigraphic stages. Three are well-constrained. Two require verification. One has been formally corrected.

180–60Jur–Cret
Pre-Rift Basement

Lahad Datu ophiolites: N-MORB basalt, gabbro, serpentinite, radiolarian cherts (125–115 Ma, K-Ar 131 ± 6 Ma). Basement nature beneath Kinabalu unresolved — 4 hypotheses at HOLD. Largely masked by later shortening and uplift.

45–35Mid-Eocene
Syn-Rift / IRU Package

PSCS subduction onset ~45–42 Ma. Half-graben syn-sedimentary faulting. Local rift inheritance exists but is overprinted by later compressional inversion — not the dominant visible signal in Kinabalu.

28–23Late Oligocene
Carbonate Window Opens · BMU/TCU Collision Onset

Carbonate platforms nucleate on PSCS flexural margin highs. BMU/TCU at ~23 Ma marks collision onset — the tectono-stratigraphic hinge between rift-drift and convergent-margin phases. Tepat and Solisip buildups initiate here.

★ BMU/TCU (~23 Ma) = collision unconformity. Previously misattributed to DRU.
23–16Early–Mid Miocene
Miocene Carbonate Peak — Survival Window

Tepat-1: 80 m carbonate buildup, 11 m gas, 37.5 m oil, ~1882 bopd. Zenith ~15–16 Ma. Success is survival-driven, not abundance-driven — the geomechanical stack (serpentinite → carbonate → FTB clastics) decouples the reservoir from tectonic destruction. Byerlee friction coefficient on serpentinite: μ = 0.20–0.30.

~16MMU
Carbonate Drowning

Middle Miocene Unconformity. Carbonate platforms drown. Clastic infill begins. This is the end of the main exploration window. Caused by propagation of BMU/TCU collision effects into the shelf over ~7 Ma — not by slab breakoff.

~15DRU
Slab Breakoff & Wedge-Top Response

Deep Regional Unconformity. Slab breakoff + wedge-top response to the earlier BMU/TCU collision. Kinabalu I-type granite intrusion 13.7–10 Ma (calc-alkaline, subduction arc magmatism confirms subduction was active). NW-SE shortening. Fold-thrust belts activated.

★ Formally corrected: DRU is NOT the collision unconformity. It is the slab breakoff response, 8 Ma after collision onset.
15–8Late Miocene
Clastic Interval — Toe-Thrust Dominance

Strong clastic progradation. Deepwater slope deposition. Sediment routing becomes the dominant basin control. Toe-thrust structural style defines the Kinabalu Basin expression. Sabah Orogeny ~15–7 Ma.

<8Present
Post-Orogeny Deepwater

Gravity-driven fold-thrust geometry. Sabah Trough is a sediment-filled foredeep — not an active trench. KL2 dataset: 8 wells, TVDSS 0–4000 m, Vavg 1800–3500 m/s, compaction Vo-K (V₀=1800, k=0.0006). Hinge-zone and deepwater-to-shelf structural complexity persist.

03

Tectonic Hypothesis

The Proto South China Sea subduction model is physics-consistent and falsifiable. It survives 5 of 8 kill tests with 3 direct validations and 0 contradictions. Three tests remain pending due to data availability, not due to contradiction.

The key insight is not that the PSCS model is proven — it is that no available evidence has killed it. Working hypothesis, not established fact.

✅ VALIDATED · OBS

C1 — PSCS oceanic crust existed

Lahad Datu N-MORB ophiolites: pillow basalt, gabbro, serpentinite, cherts. K-Ar: 131 ± 6 Ma. Radiolarian cherts: Barremian–Aptian (125–115 Ma). Low-K tholeiitic, flat REE at 10–20× chondritic = MORB signature.

✅ VALIDATED · OBS

C2 — Subduction occurred beneath NW Borneo

Kinabalu I-type granite: calc-alkaline, 13.7–10 Ma, hornblende + biotite. I-type = subduction required. S-type would falsify. Subducted slab imaged at 45–55 km depth by seismic tomography (Wu & Suppe 2018, Zhu et al. 2022, Pilia et al. 2023).

✅ VALIDATED · OBS

C3 — Positive free-air gravity offshore Sabah

Darvel Bay: 60–140 mGal. Dangerous Grounds: +50–80 mGal. Consistent with dense body (oceanic crust) beneath. No contradicting gravity signal found.

🔶 INTERPRETED

C4 — Carbonate platforms on PSCS flexural margin highs

Tepat/Solisip buildups on structural highs. Tepat-1: 80 m buildup, oil + gas producer. Badali archetype framework consistent. Platform geometry: mounded external, chaotic internal seismic facies. Alternative: volcanic seamount or attenuated continental high.

🔶 INTERPRETED · PHYSICS-DERIVED

C5 — Serpentinite as master detachment

Byerlee friction μ = 0.20–0.30 (vs 0.60–0.70 for surrounding rocks). Decouples carbonate reservoir from tectonic destruction. Carbonate Rigid Backstop AI contrast: 8,359 (g/cc · m/s). No direct serpentinite sampling from interface.

🔷 PENDING

KT-6 — Moho beneath Dangerous Grounds

NW Sabah Moho at 22–30 km (measured) is continental orogen, not PSCS domain. Dangerous Grounds Moho data must be extracted separately.

KILL: Moho ≥ 20 km → continental, not oceanic PSCS
◆ RESOLVED

KT-8 — 6–8 km reflector is thrust detachment

Not top of PSCS oceanic crust (Franke et al. 2008). Triangle zone structure. Structural boundary, not oceanic basement interface. Marginally boosts H2 (Continental Detachment).

Basement Hypotheses

Four competing hypotheses for the deep basement beneath the Kinabalu–Layang-Layang transition. All four remain alive. No hypothesis reaches the 0.70 posterior required for diagnostic promotion.

H1 — Oceanic Crust · PSCS Remnant

P = 0.30
HOLD

True oceanic lithosphere subducted and accreted under the Sabah margin. Supported by Lahad Datu ophiolites (MORB) and slab tomography. KT-8 resolution (décollement, not oceanic top) slightly weakens this path. Magnetic quiet zone is consistent but non-unique.

KILL: Basement Vp < 6.0 km/s · Granitic/metamorphic basement in well · No magnetic stripes + no oceanic Vp

H2 — Thrust Detachment over Continental Margin

P = 0.30⁺
HOLD

Deep reflector = décollement; basement is stretched continental crust. Marginally boosted by KT-8 resolution. NW Sabah Moho at 22–30 km supports continental thickness in the orogen.

KILL: Basement Vp 6.8–7.2 km/s · Magnetic isochron stripes · Sheeted dyke / pillow basalt in well

H3 — Rifted / Volcanic Continental Basement

P = 0.20
HOLD

Stretched continental crust with volcanic overprint. Requires confirmed magnetic highs (volcanic) that are not yet confirmed. No magnetic isochron stripes.

KILL: Absence of magnetic highs · Basement Vp 6.8–7.2 km/s · Pure granite/metamorphic without volcanics

H4 — Shale-Tectonic / Gravity-Driven Collapse

P = 0.20
HOLD

Deep deformation is sediment-driven, not basement-driven. Partially consistent with Kinabalu's deepwater toe-thrust architecture. Shallow structural style consistent; deep mechanism unconfirmed.

KILL: Basement Vp > 5.0 km/s at deep reflector · Reflector > 15 km · Crystalline basement in well

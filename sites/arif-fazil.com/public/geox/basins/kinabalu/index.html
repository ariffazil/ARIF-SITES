01 · Executive Argument

One machine, in sequence

Kinabalu Basin is not a simple post-rift sag or a collision product. It is the compressional and sediment-routing expression of a Proto South China Sea subduction system that drove carbonate platform development, fold-thrust architecture, and deepwater fairways — all from the same machine, in sequence.

The basin records six distinct tectonic episodes spanning ~180 Ma to present. The Miocene carbonate play — the primary exploration target — is defined not by when carbonates grew fastest, but by when they survived: a geomechanical stack of serpentinite, carbonate, and fold-thrust-belt clastics decoupled the reservoir from tectonic destruction. That survival window closes at the Middle Miocene Unconformity (~16 Ma), driven by the earlier collision at ~23 Ma (BMU/TCU), not by the Deep Regional Unconformity.

NSPW correction applied. DRU (~15 Ma) is the slab breakoff and wedge-top response — not the collision unconformity. BMU/TCU at ~23 Ma is collision onset. This shifts the causal interpretation of carbonate drowning: the window closes because collision (BMU/TCU) propagated into the shelf over ~7 Ma. DRU is a second-order response ~8 Ma later.

The basement nature beneath the basin remains unresolved. Four competing hypotheses are alive with roughly equal prior probability. None can be promoted to diagnostic without targeted acquisition. The cheapest discriminating test — depth conversion of the KT-7 reflector — is available today using existing data.

02 · Stratigraphic Inference

Eight tectono-stratigraphic stages

Three well-constrained · two require verification · one formally corrected (DRU).

180–60 Ma
Jur–Cret

Pre-Rift Basement

Lahad Datu ophiolites: N-MORB basalt, gabbro, serpentinite, radiolarian cherts (125–115 Ma, K-Ar 131 ± 6 Ma). Basement nature beneath Kinabalu unresolved — 4 hypotheses at HOLD. Largely masked by later shortening and uplift.

45–35 Ma
Mid-Eocene

Syn-Rift / IRU Package

PSCS subduction onset ~45–42 Ma. Half-graben syn-sedimentary faulting. Local rift inheritance exists but is overprinted by later compressional inversion — not the dominant visible signal in Kinabalu.

28–23 Ma
Late Oligocene

Carbonate Window Opens · BMU/TCU Collision Onset

Carbonate platforms nucleate on PSCS flexural margin highs. BMU/TCU at ~23 Ma marks collision onset — the tectono-stratigraphic hinge between rift-drift and convergent-margin phases. Tepat and Solisip buildups initiate here.

★ BMU/TCU (~23 Ma) = collision unconformity. Previously misattributed to DRU.
23–16 Ma
Early–Mid Miocene

Miocene Carbonate Peak — Survival Window

Tepat-1: 80 m carbonate buildup, 11 m gas, 37.5 m oil, ~1882 bopd. Zenith ~15–16 Ma. Success is survival-driven, not abundance-driven — the geomechanical stack (serpentinite → carbonate → FTB clastics) decouples the reservoir from tectonic destruction. Byerlee friction on serpentinite: μ = 0.20–0.30.

~16 Ma

MMU — Carbonate Drowning

Middle Miocene Unconformity. Carbonate platforms drown. Clastic infill begins. End of the main exploration window. Caused by propagation of BMU/TCU collision effects into the shelf over ~7 Ma — not by slab breakoff.

~15 Ma

DRU — Slab Breakoff & Wedge-Top Response

Deep Regional Unconformity. Slab breakoff + wedge-top response to the earlier BMU/TCU collision. Kinabalu I-type granite intrusion 13.7–10 Ma (calc-alkaline, subduction arc magmatism confirms subduction was active). NW-SE shortening. Fold-thrust belts activated.

★ Formally corrected: DRU is NOT the collision unconformity. It is the slab breakoff response, ~8 Ma after collision onset.
15–8 Ma
Late Miocene

Miocene Clastic Interval — Toe-Thrust Dominance

Strong clastic progradation. Deepwater slope deposition. Sediment routing becomes the dominant basin control. Toe-thrust structural style defines the Kinabalu Basin expression. Sabah Orogeny ~15–7 Ma.

<8 Ma → Present

Post-Orogeny Deepwater

Gravity-driven fold-thrust geometry. Sabah Trough is a sediment-filled foredeep — not an active trench. KL2 dataset: 8 wells, TVDSS 0–4000 m, V_avg 1800–3500 m/s, compaction follows Vo-K (V₀=1800, k=0.0006). Hinge-zone and deepwater-to-shelf structural complexity persist.

04 · Basement Hypotheses

Four alive · none ≥ 0.70 posterior

Deep basement beneath the Kinabalu–Layang-Layang transition. No hypothesis reaches diagnostic promotion threshold.

H1 — Oceanic Crust (PSCS remnant) HOLDP = 0.30

True oceanic lithosphere subducted/accreted under Sabah margin. Supported by Lahad Datu MORB ophiolites and slab tomography. KT-8 (décollement, not oceanic top) slightly weakens. Magnetic quiet zone consistent but non-unique.

Killer: Basement Vp < 6.0 km/s → eliminates H1. Granitic/metamorphic basement in well → eliminates H1. No magnetic stripes + no oceanic Vp → eliminates H1.

H2 — Thrust Detachment over Continental Margin HOLDP = 0.30+

Deep reflector = décollement; basement is stretched continental crust. Marginally boosted by KT-8. NW Sabah Moho 22–30 km supports continental thickness in the orogen.

Killer: Basement Vp 6.8–7.2 km/s → eliminates H2. Magnetic isochron stripes → eliminates H2. Sheeted dyke / pillow basalt in well → eliminates H2.

H3 — Rifted / Volcanic Continental Basement HOLDP = 0.20

Stretched continental crust with volcanic overprint. Magnetic highs (volcanic) not yet confirmed. Could accommodate mixed continental-volcanic if rifting generated significant intrusion.

Killer: Absence of magnetic highs → eliminates H3. Clearly oceanic Vp (6.8–7.2) → eliminates H3. Pure granite/metamorphic without volcanics → eliminates H3.

H4 — Shale-Tectonic / Gravity-Driven Collapse HOLDP = 0.20

Deep deformation sediment-driven, not basement-driven. Partially consistent with Kinabalu deepwater toe-thrust and listric growth-fault style. Requires overpressured shale at depth confirmed by well data. Deep mechanism unconfirmed.

Killer: Basement Vp > 5.0 km/s at deep reflector → eliminates H4. Reflector > 15 km depth → eliminates H4. Crystalline basement in well → eliminates H4.

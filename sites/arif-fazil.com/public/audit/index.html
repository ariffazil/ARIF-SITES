§ 5 · Verification Procedure

How to verify a record

  1. Fetch the record (e.g., /999/seal.json) and its .sig and .sha256.
  2. Compute SHA-256 of the record bytes. Compare to .sha256.
  3. Verify Ed25519 signature against the public key in /.well-known/did.json under verificationMethod.
  4. Confirm the hash chain: each record references the prior record's hash in prev_hash.

If any step fails, the record is invalid. The chamber does not adjudicate; it shows. Adjudication belongs to the sovereign (888_JUDGE) and the constitution (/constitution).

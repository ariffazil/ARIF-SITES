4. The Simulative Institution Thesis

Framework: Acemoglu & Robinson (Why Nations Fail, 2012; Nobel Prize 2024).

Definition

A simulative institution possesses genuine inclusive DNA but where extractive behaviours have captured governance while preserving the inclusive language and rituals. This is more dangerous than pure extraction because:

  • Workers believe the inclusive promise → stay too long
  • Reforms are led by extractors → loop back to extraction
  • Past achievements provide alibi for present extractive behaviour
  • Creative destruction is suppressed not by force but by bureaucratic friction

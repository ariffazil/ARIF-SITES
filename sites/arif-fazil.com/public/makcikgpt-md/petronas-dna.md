## 4 Shared Values PETRONAS (CoBE 2022)

- Loyalty — Loyal to corporation
- Integrity — Honest and upright
- Professionalism — Strive for excellence
- Cohesiveness — United, trust and respect for each other

Source: PETRONAS Code of Conduct and Business Ethics (CoBE 2022), foreword by Tengku Muhammad Taufik.

## Mission 1988 vs Purpose 2020

- Mission 1988 (Azizan era): "A business entity whose main objective is to add value to the country's oil and gas resources and contribute to the well-being of the nation."
- Statement of Purpose 2020 (Tengku Taufik era): "A progressive energy and solutions partner enriching lives for a sustainable future."

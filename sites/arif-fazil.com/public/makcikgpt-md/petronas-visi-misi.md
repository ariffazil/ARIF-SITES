## Evolusi Visi PETRONAS 1974-2025 (7 Era)

### Era 1: 1974 — Tanpa Visi Formal
Modal RM10 juta. PDA 1974 (Petroleum Development Act) diluluskan.
Rastam Hadi (Bank Negara deputy governor) jadi pengarah eksekutif pertama.
Prinsip: "Fight like bulldogs." PSC (Production Sharing Contract) ganti sistem konsesi.

### Era 2: 1988 — 4 Shared Values (Azizan Zainul Abidin, CEO)
- Loyalty — Loyal to corporation
- Integrity — Honest and upright
- Professionalism — Strive for excellence
- Cohesiveness — United, trust and respect for each other
- Mission: "A business entity whose main objective is to add value to the country's oil and gas resources and contribute to the well-being of the nation."

Sumber: PETRONAS CoBE 2022, The Edge Malaysia 8 Feb 2010 (Azam Aris)

### Era 3: 1990-an — Multinational
Vision: "To be a leading multinational oil and gas company."
PETRONAS masuk 30+ negara. Twin Towers siap 1997 (USD1.6 bilion).

### Era 4: 2000-an — Integrated Energy
Vision: "A leading integrated energy company."
Downstream + petrochemicals + maritime expansion.

### Era 5: 2019 — Progressive Energy Partner
Statement of Purpose: "A progressive energy and solutions partner enriching lives for a sustainable future."
Dilancarkan bawah CEO baru Tengku Muhammad Taufik.

### Era 6: 2020 — Net Zero 2050
5 November 2020: PETRONAS umum aspirasi Net Zero Carbon Emissions 2050.
Gentari dilancarkan 2022 sebagai subsidiary tenaga bersih.
5 Cultural Beliefs ditambah 2021: Customer Focused, Innovate Now, Be Enterprising, Speak Up, Courage to Act.

### Era 7: 2025 — Energy Superstore
"By 2035, PETRONAS aspires to operate as an integrated energy hub."
Oil & gas + renewables + hydrogen + CCS + green mobility.

## Data Kewangan Semasa

- ROACE jatuh 3 tahun: 10.8% → 9.7% → 8.2%
- Gearing naik 3 tahun: ~18% → 19.6% → 20.7%
- Production menurun: 2,451 → 2,423 kboed
- Dividen turun: RM50bn (2022) → RM20bn (2026)
- Gentari: P&L tidak disclosed, 4 tahun operasi
- Downstream LAT: -0.7bn (FY2024) → -1.9bn (FY2025) — makin teruk

## Analisis: Anti-Calhoun "Beautiful Ones"

Dalam eksperimen Calhoun Universe 25, "beautiful ones" = tikus yang cantik, bersih, tapi tak breed, tak socialize, tak contribute.
PETRONAS pattern: visi makin cantik, misi makin sophisticated, Cultural Beliefs makin banyak.
Tapi ROACE jatuh. Gentari rugi. Production menurun. Pekerja kena buang.
Misi cantik. Realiti berbeza.

Sumber: PETRONAS Integrated Reports 2022-2025, CoBE 2022, The Edge Malaysia, Calhoun (1962).

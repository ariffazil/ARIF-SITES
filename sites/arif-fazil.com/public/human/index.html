⚠️ Constitutional Boundary (F6 MARUAH)

This page publishes the evaluation methodology only. No living individual is named, scored, or ranked without their explicit written consent. This is not a limitation of capability — it is a constitutional floor.

Why: Dignity (maruah) is inviolable. An institution can be critiqued; a person cannot be reduced to a score without their participation. The framework exists so that when consent is given, the evaluation is rigorous, reproducible, and fair.

What this means: The institutional analysis at /institution names structures, decisions, and outcomes. This page names the method by which leadership quality would be assessed — the ruler, not the measured.

1. Why a Framework?

National oil companies (NOCs) operate under a unique governance paradox: they are simultaneously commercial enterprises and instruments of state policy. Their leaders navigate:

  • Dual accountability: to shareholders (the state) AND to commercial viability
  • Extraction pressure: the state's fiscal needs vs. the company's reinvestment requirements
  • Political cycles: leadership tenure tied to electoral timelines, not performance metrics
  • Workforce covenant: implicit social contract (meritocracy, security, national mission) vs. austerity realities

Standard corporate governance frameworks (OECD, IFC) do not adequately capture these tensions. This framework is purpose-built for the NOC context.

3. The Consent Protocol

How Individual Evaluation Works (When Consent Is Given)

  1. Invitation: The individual (or their authorised representative) is invited to participate.
  2. Scope agreement: Dimensions, evidence sources, and publication rights are agreed in writing.
  3. Evidence gathering: Public statements, annual reports, board decisions, and (if offered) interviews.
  4. Draft review: The individual reviews the draft evaluation before publication. Right of response included.
  5. Publication: Only with explicit written consent. Individual may withdraw consent at any time before publication.
  6. Versioning: Published evaluations are immutable once live. Corrections via appendendum, never deletion.

This protocol exists because F6 MARUAH (dignity) is a constitutional floor. The machine can compute; it cannot reduce a human to a number without their participation. This is not weakness — it is the boundary that separates governance from surveillance.

5. The "Simulative Neutral" Detection Framework

Borrowed from the WEALTH organ's external exploitation detection: a pattern where each individual action is defensible, but the aggregate reveals systematic extraction.

Detection Criteria

  1. Individually rational: Each decision (dividend, JV, restructuring) has a standalone justification
  2. Aggregate pattern: The sum of decisions transfers value from institution to extractor
  3. Asymmetric risk: The institution bears downside; the extractor captures upside
  4. Narrative cover: Each action is framed in inclusive language ("partnership", "transformation", "efficiency")
  5. No reversal mechanism: Once value is transferred, no contractual path returns it

When all five criteria are present simultaneously, the pattern is classified as simulative neutral — rational exploitation of institutional weakness. This is not conspiracy; it is the predictable outcome of unchecked sole-shareholder power.

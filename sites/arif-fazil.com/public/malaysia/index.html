How to read this organ
What the sovereign pulse measures

The composite PULSE (0–100) is a weighted blend of three sub-scores — fiscal capacity, economic engine, and institutions & demographics — each calculated from three tripwires measuring distance from a breaking threshold. It does not predict crisis. It measures the gap between current readings and the safe/trip anchors.

How sub-scores are calculated

Each tripwire t has three anchors: now (current reading), trip (breaking threshold), and safe (healthy anchor). The score is distance-to-trip normalized to 0–100. The layer score is the mean of its three tripwire scores. The composite is: PULSE = 0.40·fiscal capacity + 0.35·economic engine + 0.25·institutions & demographics.

About GEOX

GEOX is the Earth Intelligence organ of the arifOS federation. It provides MCP tools for geological interpretation, petrophysical analysis, seismic processing, and geospatial reasoning. GEOX operates under the constitutional floors (F1–F13) and feeds evidence to the WEALTH and WELL organs for cross-domain intelligence.

Earnings Model Framework

Projected Revenue  = Σ (Segment Volume × Benchmark Price × Realisation Discount/Premium)
Projected EBITDA   = Revenue × EBITDA Margin (segment-specific, cycle-adjusted)
Projected PAT      = EBITDA − D&A − Financing Costs − Tax (PITA ~38% marginal)
Projected CFFO     = PAT + D&A ± Working Capital ± Other
Free Cash Flow     = CFFO − CAPEX − Dividends − Minority Interests
Net Cash/(Debt)    = Prior Net Cash + FCF + Net Borrowings − Debt Repayments

9. Sovereign Extraction Model — The Fiscal Trap

PETRONAS is Malaysia's shadow sovereign balance sheet. The government extracts via annual dividend + petroleum income tax — combined RM42B/year. This exceeds free cash flow after maintenance CAPEX.

SOVEREIGN CONTEXT. The declining petroleum revenue share (28%→12.5%) masks absolute dependency. RM43B is 12.5% of federal revenue. Federal debt: RM1.3T (63.1% GDP in Q1 2026, down from 65.2%). Statutory debt ceiling: 65%. FRA Act 850 mandates deficit below 3% and debt below 60% medium-term. Sovereign rating: Moody's A3 stable (affirmed 14 Jul 2026) — "fastest-growing among A-rated peers." Risks are balanced, not negative watch. There is no fiscal space for a PETRONAS bailout, but the sovereign backdrop is stable, not deteriorating. PETRONAS's dividend is the government's fiscal oxygen — and the oxygen supply is compressing, not collapsing.

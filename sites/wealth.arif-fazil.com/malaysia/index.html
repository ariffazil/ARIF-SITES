How to read this organ
Pulse computation
What the sovereign pulse measures

The composite PULSE (0–100) is a weighted blend of three sub-scores — BODY (fiscal solvency), ENGINE (economic motor), and SOUL (institutional DNA) — each computed from three tripwires measuring distance from a breaking threshold. It does not predict crisis. It measures the gap between current readings and the safe/trip anchors.

How sub-scores are computed

Each tripwire t has three anchors: now (current reading), trip (breaking threshold), and safe (healthy anchor). The score is distance-to-trip normalized to 0–100. The layer score is the mean of its three tripwire scores. The composite is: PULSE = 0.40·BODY + 0.35·ENGINE + 0.25·SOUL.

Epistemic honesty

BODY tripwires (#1–3) are EVIDENCE — grounded in Bank Negara, MoF, and DOSM published data. ENGINE tripwires (#4–6) are MIXED — reported data with interpretation. SOUL tripwires (#7–9) are INTERPRET — indices requiring judgment. The composite PULSE inherits the weakest tag: INTERPRET. The organ cannot be more objective than the judgments encoded in its safe values.

Relationship to PETRONAS VITALS

PETRONAS VITALS is the corporate institutional pulse — "one body, two lungs." It measures the company that funds ~20% of federal revenue.

MALAYSIA VITALS is the federal sovereign pulse — "one nation, five pillars." It measures the nation that owns the company.

They are two distinct organisms, cross-linked. PETRONAS fiscal stress flows into Malaysia BODY (#1–3). Malaysia institutional decay flows into PETRONAS SOUL (#7–9). Read both. Seal separately.
